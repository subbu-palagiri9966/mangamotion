Built for legitimate creation

Reference the series.
Don’t steal the series.

MangaMotion should support original, public-domain and licensed comics. Existing anime clips, actor voices and soundtracks cannot be copied into a public generator without the rights holders’ and performers’ permission.

That is why the product accepts an explicit authorised reference pack instead of scraping released episodes.
